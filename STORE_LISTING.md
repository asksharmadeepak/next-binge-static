# NextBinge — Google Play listing (paste into Play Console)

Live app: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nextbinge.app

## Category

1. Open Play Console → **Store presence** → **Main store listing** (or **App content** / category settings as shown in your console).
2. Change category from **Productivity** to **Entertainment**.
3. Save.

Content rating can stay **Everyone** if kids-friendly mode remains optional and the listing stays family-safe.

## Full description

```
NextBinge helps you choose something to watch without endless scrolling.

Get one clear recommendation in seconds, tuned to your language, region, and kids-friendly preference.

HOW IT WORKS
• Flip the card for tonight’s pick
• Swipe or shake for another option (on supported devices)
• Save titles to your watchlist
• Open details for poster, overview, and richer info when available

WHY NEXTBINGE
• Fast picks — one focused flow instead of long lists
• Your preferences — language, region, and family-friendly mode
• Built for quick decisions — save for later or dig deeper when you want more

Note: Recommendations use public movie/TV information. Streaming availability can vary by region.
```
