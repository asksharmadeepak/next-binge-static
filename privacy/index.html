Privacy policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026 · Contact: deepsharma989@gmail.com

This policy describes how the NextBinge mobile application (“the App”) handles information. The marketing site for this product may appear under the funnel name WhatWatchNext; the App itself is NextBinge.

Summary

The App helps you discover titles to watch. It may use optional sign-in, local preferences, and third-party services (such as The Movie Database (TMDB)) to show film and TV metadata. We do not sell your personal data.

Information you provide

  • Account (optional): If you sign in, your identity provider (e.g. Firebase / Google) may process email or profile data according to their policies.
  • Watchlist: Titles you save may be stored with your account or on your device, depending on how the App is configured.

Information collected automatically

  • App preferences: Such as language, region, or family-friendly mode, stored on the device and/or your account.

Third-party services

  • TMDB: The App requests movie and TV information from TMDB. See TMDB’s privacy policy.
  • Firebase / Google: Optional authentication and related backend services may process account data under Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The App may offer a kids-friendly recommendation mode. It is not directed at children under 13 in a way that intentionally collects personal information beyond what is needed for the service.

Data retention & deletion

Local preferences stay on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. If you use an account-backed watchlist, contact us to request deletion of that data. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact deepsharma989@gmail.com.
